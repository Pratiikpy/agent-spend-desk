# Divergent ideas run (ADHD method), 2026-10-18

Question: is the Decision Desk the best idea with no time and no build limit, and which framings could beat it. Method: five isolated idea branches (regulator, hostile competitor, markets, infinite budget, remove-the-assumption), six ideas each, then three deepened. Choices below are the author's judgment, not measurements.

## Clusters
1. **Replay and rule governance** (recall ledger, counterfactual sandbox, living policy twin, decision autopsy, policy diff radar, counterfactual court): chosen as the star upgrade.
2. **Contradiction and truth substrate** (contradiction court, silent witness, federated truth auditor, disagreement engine): Track 1 core; a "no decision" product has little to demo.
3. **Exchanges and markets** (clearing house, evidence futures, reverse auction, dispute escrow, decision insurance, calibration exchange): the clearing house is kept; the token and futures variants are traps (regulatory risk, gimmick).
4. **Compiler or platform** (reasoning-architecture compiler, proposed twice): kept as a long-term direction.
5. **Auditing other agents** (drift witness, agent underwriter, red-team, adversarial twin, removing the human reviewer): partly built in already (Skeptic and Advocate); rivals now crowd this.
6. **The affected person's side** (appeal drafting for a denied person): a different buyer; later.

## Deepened
- **Living Policy Twin (star):** replay all history against a proposed rule, model or policy change; classify flips (approve to route, route to approve); give the minimal-cause fact for each flip; prove the change only restricts, or list the exact widening region for human sign-off; sign an impact dossier; the same registry answers appeals and regulator reconstruction. Load-bearing risk: replay fidelity (point-in-time data, pinned model steps); the baseline must reproduce recorded decisions at about 100%, or every diff is noise. First step: a baseline-fidelity harness.
- **Neutral clearing house:** boundary nodes return only claim, quote span, record hash and signature, never rows; a kernel verifies quotes and evaluates machine-checkable coverage contracts; both sides verify offline. Risk: leakage through repeated queries, and the legal weight of published contracts (HIPAA minimum-necessary is **NOT VERIFIED**). First step: a criteria-contract schema with a symbolic evaluator on one public policy.
- **Reasoning-architecture compiler:** a document in, a verified agent graph out (atoms with quote spans, a typed intermediate form, fixed kernel templates, termination by acyclic graph, tests generated from the atoms, a signed conformance report). Risk: semantic fidelity of the compile step; the tests must be independent of the reading they test (two independent compilers whose differences become questions). First step: hand-write the target form for one real policy.

## Provocation kept
A stranger verifies a dossier on their own machine with the network cable pulled: the demo's strongest moment.
