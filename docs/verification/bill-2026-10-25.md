# The bill and its export (2026-10-25)

`desk bill [--month 2026-09 | --since D --until D] [--export csv|json] [--output FILE]`, and the Bill screen, read one query, so the text, the export and the screen cannot disagree.

## What the bill says

- The headline in estimated dollars at list price, with the sentence "an estimate from logged tokens, not an invoice".
- **Which kind of figure stands behind the dollars.** An invoice, the tool's own running cost and our estimate are three measurements of the same spend, not parts of one total. So the line says, of the estimated dollars, how many fall in a month an invoice covers, how many in a process whose tool recorded its own cost, and how many have only our estimate; each call is counted once under the highest-ranked kind available to it. Calls on a model with no price are counted in a separate figure and never given a share or a dollar.
- Billed against estimated for each vendor month, and why a month is or is not reconciled; what it bought by landing state with the dollars resting on a weak link apart; cost per landed session; the causes; the pricing rules in force; the price check where two sources disagree; models and projects.
- Without an invoice there is no billed figure, and the bill says what importing one would change.

## The export

One row per day, project, model, landing state and class, with the tokens and the dollars: `estimated` rows from the calls, `unpriced` rows with the tokens and no amount, and `billed` rows from the imported invoices (in their own currency), billed first. CSV is CRLF with quoting; a cell a spreadsheet would read as a formula (a project or model name that begins with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@`) is made plain text with a leading apostrophe. JSON carries the same lines and the whole bill.

## Every figure opens its rows

On the Bill screen the spend, each landing state, each day, each model and each project open the Sessions screen narrowed to the same period and the same state, day, model or project, with a line saying what the rows add up to.

## What was run

- `tests/desk/bill.test.ts` (11): the mix with no invoice, with an invoice covering a month (billed), and with a tool-recorded process (reported), each call once and the three shares adding to the total; the text for a month and for any dates; the refusals (a bad month, a month with dates, a bad date, since after until, an empty month, an unknown export kind); the export rows adding up to the bill to the micro-dollar; the CSV header, CRLF, quoting, the spreadsheet-formula guard, an unpriced row with no amount; billed rows first with their currency; a written file.
- `tests/desk/ui/brief.test.ts`: every model, project, landing state and day of the bill reproduces to the micro-dollar from the sessions its link opens.
- Run on this machine for September 2026: 129 lines, estimated amounts summing to $14,187.849291, which is the bill's $14,187.85; 18 unpriced lines.

## Not covered

- The "reported" share has only been exercised on a constructed process; on this machine no log carries the tool's own cost, so it reads 0%.
- Idle and shared spend are not allocated to people or projects (DF-095); the export and the bill carry what the calls say.
- An invoice in a currency other than USD appears as billed rows in its own currency, and the month is unreconciled; it is not converted.
- The export has not been opened in a spreadsheet application; the guard follows the published rule for formula injection in CSV (a leading apostrophe), which is NOT VERIFIED against any one application.
