# Earned autonomy (2026-10-25)

Feature DF-093. A kind of work starts supervised: at a soft limit the gate asks a person. It can earn the right to go on without asking, and loses it the moment it stops deserving it. `desk autonomy status | evaluate [--apply] | approve | block | unblock | history`, a section of the Policy screen, and a tier the gate reads at every decision.

## What it does

- **The unit and the kinds.** A run is one agent session. A kind of work is the type of its task, the branch prefix: `feat`, `fix`, `chore`, or `branch` for a branch with no prefix and `session` for work with no label. The gate types a hook request by the branch of the folder it comes from (read from `.git/HEAD`, no process started, held for five seconds), so a policy rule on `task_type` and earned autonomy mean the same thing in the gate as in the ledger. A folder that is not in a repository stays `code`, as every hook request was before.
- **What counts.** A run is a success when its work is merged, at least 14 days old, and never reverted; a failure when a commit of it was reverted (at any age), or when it sat committed and unmerged for 14 days. A run still inside its 14 days, and a run never tied to a commit, are counted apart and are in neither number. A low-confidence link is a guess and decides nothing. Age is taken as of the later of the clock and the ledger's last call, so a ledger of recorded sessions is judged as of its own last day.
- **The tiers.** `blocked`, `supervised` (the default), `guarded_auto`, `unassisted_auto`. A supervised kind with at least 30 finished runs, a Wilson 95% lower bound of its success rate of at least 90%, and no revert in the last 14 days is **proposed** for `guarded_auto`. (Thirty clean runs give a lower bound of 88.6%; it takes 35.) The owner approves by giving the first eight characters of the digest of the evidence as it stands, which they can only have from reading it; if the evidence has moved since (a run finished, a revert), the code no longer matches and nothing is promoted. A promoted kind is **demoted at once, automatically,** when a commit of it was reverted in the last 7 days, or its lower bound falls under 85% over at least 30 runs. A block is the owner's alone to set and lift, and lifting returns the kind to supervised. The Desk never promotes by itself and never moves anything past `guarded_auto`.
- **In the gate.** For a spend action (a model call or a tool call) the gate does not ask at a soft limit for a promoted kind, and records the reason `earned_autonomy`; a blocked kind is denied with `workflow_blocked`. A hard cap, a forbidden action, a paused person, a closed checkpoint and an approval-gated action such as raising a cap are untouched. The tiers live in the gate's own database, set only through the owner's route (`/v1/admin/autonomy`, owner token), and read at every decision, so a demotion takes effect on the next call.
- **The record.** Each proposal, promotion, demotion and block is a row in `autonomy_event` with the evidence (counts, bound, the revert and its commit) and its digest. Schema 25 also records when a commit was reverted, as the time of the revert commit.

## What ran

- `tests/desk/autonomy.test.ts` (28): judging a run (success at 14 days and not at 13.9, a revert at any age, an unmerged commit); the thresholds (29 runs held, 30 clean held at 88.6%, 34 held, 35 proposed, one failure among 120 still proposed, 8 among 108 held); a recent revert holding a promotion for 14 days; a promoted kind demoted by a revert within 7 days and by a bound under 85%, and not by 10 runs of 5; a block untouched; the digest's sensitivity; from a ledger (counts, unsettled and untied runs, a low-confidence link left out, a proposal recorded once, a wrong, stale or foreign code refused, a planted revert demoting at once with its reason, a block and its lifting); the commands; the scan recording the time of a revert on a real repository; typing a folder by its branch; in the gate (a soft ask for a supervised kind, none for a promoted one, asking again on the very next call after a demotion, a hard cap and an approval-gated action not turned into an allow, a blocked kind refused, the hook path typed by branch); the owner's route (a 403 without the owner token, validation, persistence across a restart on the same database); and the whole path over HTTP: promote in the ledger, push to a running gate, the next call is not asked, plant a revert, demote, the call after that is asked.
- `tests/desk/ui/autonomy-ui.test.ts` (2): the Policy screen's data and section.
- On the constructed sample ledger (a copy), 12 kinds of work have 35 to 47 finished runs each, with success rates of 76% to 94% and lower bounds of 61% to 81%, so **none is proposed**: the 90% bound is not cleared by any. On this machine's own ledger there are two kinds (`branch`, `session`), no finished run, and 425 runs not tied to a commit, so nothing can be judged.

## Not covered

- **`unassisted_auto` has no effect of its own.** It is accepted, recorded and shown, the owner can set it, and the gate treats it as `guarded_auto`. The specification names the tier and gives no rule for what it adds; none was invented.
- A promotion is proposed from the ledger and applied when the owner approves; the gate learns of it when `desk autonomy approve` (or `evaluate --apply`) pushes to it with `DESK_GATE_OWNER_TOKEN`. If the gate is not reachable, the ledger holds the change and the command says the gate was not told and exits non-zero; **a demotion is only as instant as the next `evaluate --apply`**, which something must run on a schedule (no schedule is installed by this).
- The kind of work is the branch prefix. A team that names branches `eng03/…` by engineer gets a kind per engineer, which is what the constructed sample does; a kind per workflow needs branch names that carry one, or labels (`desk task`).
- Revert time comes from the revert commit's author date. A revert done by reset or force-push leaves no commit and is not seen; a commit reverted before the Desk scanned that repository has a time only if the revert commit is still in the history.
- The success rate is about landing, not about quality: a merged change that was wrong but never reverted is a success.
- The thresholds (30, 90%, 85%, 14 and 7 days) are the specification's. They were not tuned or checked against how often a promoted kind then goes wrong; there is no such record yet.
- The gate was tested in process and over its HTTP route on Windows; a promoted kind was not run for a period with a real agent.
